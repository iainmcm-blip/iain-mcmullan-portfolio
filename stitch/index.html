    How I work 5 steps

    From the brief to the live site, one pair of hands.

    Most projects pass through a strategist, a copywriter, a designer and a developer, and lose something at every hand-off. Mine don't, because all four are me.

    1. 01

      The brief

      We talk it through, or you send the deck you already have. I come back with what I'd do and what it would take.

    2. 02

      The strategy

      One page: who it's for, what they should think and do, and the idea that gets them there.

    3. 03

      The words

      I write the copy myself, from the site and the emails to the programme or the book.

    4. 04

      The build

      I design and build the site, and you review it on a live link while it grows.

    5. 05

      The launch

      It goes live, then the follow-through: fixes, measurement and the next round of content.
