Five ways to land the masthead

Pick the one that feels like you.

  1. 01Light tableTwelve prints drop onto paper and develop; the pencil circles three with their figures. Hover lifts a print and plays its film.
  2. 02Projector gateOne strip of the work runs through a gate; the frame in the gate plays in colour. Scroll speeds it up, hover stops it.
  3. 03Living gridEleven pale tiles; each develops and plays under the cursor, and one develops on its own every few seconds.
  4. 04Type windowThe films play through huge letterforms, one after another.
  5. 05Rotating developFull screen, but each film develops over the last and holds four seconds.
