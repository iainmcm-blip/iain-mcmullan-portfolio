Loyalty
  1. The problem

    Thousands of Emirates Skywards members were less than two flights from the next tier, and most weren't aware of it.

  2. The approach

    A data-driven CRM programme that told every near-tier member exactly how close they were, with personalised example flights drawn from their own travel pattern.

  3. The result

    Over 1,500 members upgraded to the next tier across the 2014–2015 programme run, generating USD 2M+ in incremental revenue. Led end to end as the Skywards CRM and campaign owner.

Brand Launch
  1. The problem

    Launch the Emirates Flight Training Academy, mid-construction, into an aviation-education category that was visually and tonally homogenous.

  2. The approach

    'Go Beyond.' A brand essence that differentiated at every level — from founding philosophy to the typeface on the wall — and read as a school built to lead the category, not join it.

  3. The result

    100% of the inaugural cadet cohort filled in under two months from launch. Owned client-side end to end — from agency selection through the brand guidelines to the VP rollout.

About

The strategy, the ideas
and the follow-through.

I have spent 20+ years on both sides of the brief: in-house at Emirates and AIA, and running strategy at agencies for Hilton, UOB and Malaysia Airlines. Working freelance, the person who writes your strategy also writes the copy and builds the site, so nothing gets lost in the hand-offs. A faster ship doesn't help if the compass is broken, so I fix the compass first. I also read more ancient history than is strictly useful, which shows up in the work more often than you would expect.

Singapore-based, working across APAC and the Middle East. Open to the right senior in-house role too.

Off the clock

The other discipline.

Most of what I know about chasing a standard, I learned in a throwing circle. After a 22-year break, I picked up the shot put again and started training seriously, with making the final 8 in the World Masters' Athletics Championships as the goal. Two national titles and an Asian Masters silver came on the way to Daegu.

Away from sport, my attention is taken by rather older disciplines: ancient history, languages, rhetoric and philosophy. They are not a break from the work, they are where the judgement, creativity and even wisdom come from. Learning classical Latin made me a better writer, and knowing the details of Stoic philosophy has made me a happier human.
